# Brewing Guide

Evoker's Omen uses a five-level brewing progression.

![Evoker's Omen brewing progression](assets/brewing-progression.webp)

## Level I

1. Brew an **Awkward Potion** with an **Ominous Bottle I** to create the Level I Essence.
2. Brew that Essence with an **Emerald**.
3. The result is **Evoker's Omen I**.

## Level II

1. Brew an **Awkward Potion** with an **Ominous Bottle II** to create the Level II Essence.
2. Brew that Essence with a genuine **Ominous Banner**.
3. The result is **Evoker's Omen II**.

A normal white banner does not work.

## Level III

1. Brew an **Awkward Potion** with an **Ominous Bottle III** to create the Level III Essence.
2. Add an **Emerald** and a genuine **Ominous Banner**.
3. These two ingredients can be added in either order.
4. The result is **Evoker's Omen III**.

## Level IV

1. Brew an **Awkward Potion** with **Evoker's Omen III** to create the Level IV Essence.
2. Brew that Essence with an **Emerald Block**.
3. The result is **Evoker's Omen IV**.

## Level V

1. Brew an **Awkward Potion** with **Evoker's Omen IV** to create the Level V Essence.
2. Brew that Essence with a **Totem of Undying**.
3. The result is **Evoker's Omen V**.

## Important details

- Ominous Bottle I, II, and III are accepted for the first three levels.
- Ominous Bottle IV and V are intentionally rejected by this progression.
- Levels II and III require the genuine Ominous Banner pattern.
- The regular Essence potions may appear in Creative, but automatic Splash, Lingering, and Tipped Arrow Essence variants are hidden.
