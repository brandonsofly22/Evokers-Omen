# Drops & Rewards

Evoker's Omen raids use level-based emerald rewards and can drop Evoker's Omen items.

## Emeralds

Custom raid Evokers replace their vanilla emerald roll with these ranges:

| Omen level | Emeralds |
| --- | ---: |
| I | 1–5 |
| II | 2–5 |
| III | 2–6 |
| IV | 3–6 |
| V | 3–7 |

Other vanilla loot is left untouched.

## Evoker's Omen drops

Each Evoker can drop **at most one** Evoker's Omen item.

A normal Evoker — including world-spawned, summoned, and vanilla-raid Evokers — has a **5% chance** to drop Evoker's Omen I.

For custom raid Evokers, the combined chance rises with raid level:

| Custom raid level | Total Omen drop chance |
| --- | ---: |
| I | 11.5% |
| II | 11.5% |
| III | 11.5% |
| IV | 11.5% |
| V | 11.5% |

The possible tier depends on the custom raid level: higher-level raids unlock chances for higher-level Omen drops. The roll is combined so a single Evoker never drops multiple Omen items.
