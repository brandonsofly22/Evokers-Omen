# FAQ & Troubleshooting

## Does Evoker's Omen replace normal raids?

No. Vanilla Ominous Bottles continue to trigger normal vanilla raids. The Evoker-only behavior is tied to the custom Evoker's Omen system.

## Does it change normal Evokers?

Normal world-spawned and summoned Evokers keep their normal behavior. They can have a small chance to drop Evoker's Omen I, but they are not treated as custom raid Evokers.

## Why won't Ominous Bottle IV or V brew the first step?

That is intentional. The first three Omen levels use Ominous Bottle I–III. Levels IV and V progress from Evoker's Omen III and IV instead.

## Why won't my banner work for Level II or III?

The recipe checks for the genuine Ominous Banner pattern. A plain white banner or a lookalike banner will not work.

## Where are the items in Creative?

Evoker's Omen I–V are in **Food & Drinks**.

## Can I use /summon normally?

Yes. Evoker's Omen does not replace ordinary `/summon` behavior.

## My game will not launch

Check that you are using:

- Minecraft 26.2
- Fabric Loader 0.19.3 or newer
- Fabric API 0.160.0+26.2
- Java 25 or newer

Also make sure you installed `evokers-omen-1.0.0.jar`, not the sources JAR.

## Reporting a bug

Please report reproducible issues through the project's GitHub issue tracker and include what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead, and relevant logs when available.
