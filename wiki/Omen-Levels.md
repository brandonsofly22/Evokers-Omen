# Evoker's Omen Levels

Evoker's Omen has five levels. Each level uses its own item texture and increases the custom raid's difficulty.

![Evoker's Omen I through V](assets/omen-levels.webp)

| Level | Extra raid scaling | Custom emerald range per raid Evoker |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| I | Baseline | 1–5 |
| II | +25% | 2–5 |
| III | +50% | 2–6 |
| IV | +75% | 3–6 |
| V | +100% | 3–7 |

The scaling percentages describe the intended increase in Evoker count relative to the custom Level I raid. Actual wave totals still depend on Minecraft's underlying raid composition.

All five Evoker's Omen items stack to 64.

## Creative inventory

The actual Evoker's Omen I–V items are placed in the **Food & Drinks** Creative tab.
