# Evoker's Omen Wiki

Welcome to the official documentation for **Evoker's Omen**.

Evoker's Omen adds a five-level brewing progression that lets players trigger increasingly difficult Evoker-only raids while leaving vanilla Ominous Bottles and vanilla raids unchanged.

> Documentation for version 1.0.0 — Minecraft 26.2, Fabric.

## At a glance

- Five levels of Evoker's Omen
- Custom brewing progression
- Evoker-only custom raids
- Increasing raid difficulty at higher Omen levels
- Scaling emerald rewards
- Evoker's Omen item drops
- Vanilla Ominous Bottles and vanilla raids remain unchanged
- Custom textures for all five levels
